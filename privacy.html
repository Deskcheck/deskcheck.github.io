Privacy

This page explains what happens to the data you send through the “Join the beta” form on deskcheck.pro, and what the site and the form touch while you look at them.

Who we are

Deskcheck is made by Ihor Bielykh, sole proprietor, Kryvyi Rih, Ukraine. He decides how your data is used (the data controller). Write to hello@deskcheck.pro about anything on this page.

What we collect

  • Your country of residence and that you are not a citizen of Russia or Belarus.
  • Your name, email address and, if you give them, your Telegram name and WhatsApp number, plus how you want us to contact you.
  • About your project: what kind of project it is, the company name, its country and website for a work project, and a project link if you give one. Company details you enter and then remove by choosing another kind of project may still be kept with your sign-up.
  • Your stack: programming languages, UI frameworks, versions, components and the controls that matter to you.
  • Your setup: the AI coding agent you use, whether you have Windows with Hyper-V, and how you heard about Deskcheck.
  • Your consent and whether you want news.
  • When you open deskcheck.pro, the page loads Tally's form script, so Tally receives what any website gets from your browser (your IP address and browser details) and the address of the page, even if you never use the form. Google reCAPTCHA receives the same when you reach the form's last page. While you fill the form in, Tally keeps your unfinished answers in your own browser so you can continue later; they leave your browser only when you press “Join the beta”.

Why

  • To contact you about the Deskcheck beta through the channel you chose.
  • To see which applications, frameworks and components the beta needs to support first.
  • To send you Deskcheck news, only if you ticked that box.

We use your sign-up on the basis of your consent, which you give with the checkbox in the form. The country and citizenship questions also serve our legitimate interest in keeping Deskcheck away from Russia and Belarus, and we check them before you reach the consent box; the form refuses automatically, and a sign-up that fails the check is deleted within a week, with its notification email. The form's questions marked as required are needed to join the beta. We do not sell your data and share it with no one beyond the services named below.

Where it is kept

  • The form runs on Tally (Tally BV, Belgium), which stores the answers for us as our processor.
  • The form uses Google reCAPTCHA to stop spam; Google receives technical data about your browser for that check (Google's privacy policy).
  • A copy of each sign-up reaches our mailbox, a Gmail account that Google provides under its own terms. Mail you send to hello@deskcheck.pro is forwarded there by our domain provider, Spaceship (United States).
  • If you asked for news, your name and email address also go to our news list, a private Google Sheets spreadsheet in the same Google account.

We work from Ukraine, so your data is used outside the EU. Ukraine has no EU adequacy decision, so the protection there may differ from the EU's. Google and Spaceship handle it in the United States; Google does so under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long

Your sign-up and its notification email: until the beta ends or you ask us to delete it, and no longer than 24 months after you sign up; we delete both together. If you asked for news, your name and email address stay on the news list until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, ask us to restrict how we use it, object to its use, and withdraw your consent at any time; withdrawing does not undo what was done before. Write to hello@deskcheck.pro. Your data is protected by the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”, and, if you are in the EU or the EEA, by the GDPR; you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Deskcheck is not available in Russia or Belarus, or to their citizens.
